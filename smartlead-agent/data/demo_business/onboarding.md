# Onboarding

## After A Lead Is Captured

The team reviews the lead details, checks service fit, and decides whether to schedule discovery.

## Discovery Call

The discovery call confirms the business type, current marketing, target customers, budget range, timeline, and decision criteria.

## Proposal

After discovery, the team prepares a proposal with scope, pricing, timeline, and next steps.

## Kickoff

Kickoff includes access collection, project setup, communication cadence, and reporting expectations.

## Required Information

Useful intake details include name, email, phone, business type, website URL, service interest, budget, timeline, goals, and current marketing channels.
