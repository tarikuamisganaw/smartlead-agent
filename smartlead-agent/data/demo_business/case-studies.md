# Case Studies

## Gym SEO Campaign

A local gym improved search visibility by targeting neighborhood fitness keywords, optimizing its Google Business Profile, and publishing service pages for personal training and group classes.

## Ecommerce Paid Ads Campaign

An ecommerce brand used paid ads with improved product landing pages, weekly budget reviews, and conversion tracking to identify profitable campaigns.

## Real Estate Lead Generation Campaign

A real estate team used landing pages, local SEO, and follow-up automation to capture buyer and seller inquiries from targeted neighborhoods.
