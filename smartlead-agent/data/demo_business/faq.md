# FAQ

## How long does onboarding take?

Most projects can start within one to two weeks after a discovery call and signed proposal.

## Do you work with small businesses?

Yes. SmartLead Agent is designed around local and small business sales workflows.

## How do discovery calls work?

Discovery calls review the business goal, current website or campaigns, target customers, budget range, timeline, and decision process.

## Can you help with existing websites?

Yes. We can improve an existing site or plan a rebuild if that is more practical.

## What information should clients provide?

Clients should provide their name, email, phone number, business type, service interest, budget range, timeline, website URL if available, and the main outcome they want.
